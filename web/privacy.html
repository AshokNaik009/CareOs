Rafeeq privacy policy

Last updated 25 September 2026. Rafeeq is a hackathon demo of a personal health agent. It is not a medical service and is not intended for real patient care.

What we access

If you connect a WHOOP account, Rafeeq requests read-only access to your WHOOP profile, body measurements, recovery, sleep, cycles and workouts, only for the scopes you approve on WHOOP's consent screen.

How we use it

The data is used only to show your wearable signals in the demo and to let the Rafeeq assistant discuss them with you. When you use the voice or text assistant, the relevant health context is sent to the AI providers that power it (ElevenLabs, and Groq or OpenRouter for summaries) to generate responses.

Optional Live Health contact alerts

Live Health analysis is separate from the demo assistants. If you explicitly enable contact alerts, your name, chosen contact’s name and phone number, and the matched WHOOP readings with their timestamps are sent to Retell AI to place an automated wellness check-in call. Only add a contact who has agreed to these calls. Blood pressure and abnormal-rhythm detection are not available through this integration. These delayed wearable alerts are not an emergency service.

Contact details, alert preferences, and recent call status are stored in server memory for your Live Health session, which lasts at most 24 hours. Sign-out, disconnect, expiry, or a server restart stops further monitoring and clears session data. Minimal WHOOP account and reading identifiers used to prevent duplicate calls may remain in memory for up to 48 hours after a call attempt. Closing a browser tab does not end monitoring. Pausing cannot cancel a call already submitted to Retell. Retell and telephony providers may retain call data according to their policies and the agent’s recording and retention settings; clearing this app’s memory does not delete their records.

Storage and retention

WHOOP tokens and data are held in server memory only. Nothing is written to a database, and everything is erased when the server restarts. We do not sell, rent or share your data for advertising.

Your choices

You can revoke Rafeeq's access at any time from your WHOOP account settings. Revoking access stops any further data from being read.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the Rafeeq team through the contact listed on our WHOOP developer app.
